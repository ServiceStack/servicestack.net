**Gemini RAG** turns the files, documentation repositories and websites you already maintain into managed knowledge bases inside your ServiceStack App. Import content into isolated **File Stores**, preview what changed before anything is embedded, then curate it with metadata so each experience only retrieves the category, product, version and status relevant to it - no crawler, vector database, sync jobs or admin UI to assemble first.

One import then publishes two public experiences to any site with a single script tag: a citation-backed **Website Assistant** whose answers link to the evidence behind every claim, and an instant `⌘K` **Website Search** answered entirely by your own database at **no cost per query**. Search analytics surface what visitors look for and can't find, while conversations, catalogues and optional first-party analytics stay in your App's database.
